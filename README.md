## 📖 About the Series

**One Piece** is a Japanese *manga* and *anime* series written and illustrated by
**Eiichiro Oda**. It follows **Monkey D. Luffy** and his crew, the *Straw Hat Pirates*,
as they sail the Grand Line in search of the legendary treasure called **One Piece**.

- **Genre:** Adventure, Fantasy, Comedy, Drama
- **Author:** Eiichiro Oda
- **Manga run:** 1997 – present
- **Anime run:** 1999 – present

### The Premise

After eating a mysterious **Devil Fruit**, Luffy's body gains the properties of
*rubber*. He gathers a crew of unique dreamers and sets out to become the
**Pirate King**.

## ⭐ Why I Like It

1. **World-building** — every island feels alive and connected to the larger story.
2. **Characters** — each crew member has a dream, a flaw, and a reason to grow.
3. **Emotion** — it can be hilarious one chapter and heartbreaking the next.
4. **Payoff** — small details from hundreds of chapters ago come back in a big way.

> *"No matter how deep the night, it always turns to day, eventually."*
